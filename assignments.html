How grades add up

Homework

Five problem sets, 5% each. Submit a rendered report (PDF/HTML) plus your .Rmd/.qmd or .ipynb.

HW1 · Lectures 1–3

Exploration, regression & partitioning

West Roxbury housing: EDA with at least three informative plots; multiple linear regression for TOTAL VALUE; train/validation split; RMSE and MAE; a short paragraph on what the model can and cannot be used for.

HW2 · Lectures 4–5

Logistic regression & classifier evaluation

Universal Bank: model personal-loan acceptance, interpret odds ratios, build confusion matrices at cutoffs of 0.5 and 0.2, and draw ROC and lift charts. Recommend a cutoff for a marketing campaign and justify it.

HW3 · Lectures 6–7

Variable selection, regularization & PCA

Toyota Corolla: compare stepwise selection, ridge, lasso and elastic net using cross-validated RMSE. Cereals: run PCA with and without scaling, read the loadings and decide how many components to keep.

HW4 · Lectures 8–10

kNN, trees & neural networks

One classification problem, three models: tune k by cross-validation, prune a tree and extract its rules, and tune a single-hidden-layer network (size and decay). Compare all three on the same test set.

HW5 · Lectures 11–13

Ensembles, rare events & clustering

Random forest and boosting with variable importance; an imbalanced-class problem handled with oversampling and a cost-based cutoff; hierarchical and k-means clustering of the Utilities data with cluster profiles.

Dataset ideas

UCI Machine Learning Repository, Kaggle, data.gov, the CDC, the City of Toledo open data portal, or your own research data (with permission). As a reference point, a 2023 team compared kNN, a pruned tree, a neural network and a random forest on the Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer data; the neural net had the best test accuracy (0.977). Aim for a dataset with at least ~500 rows and a clear outcome to predict.
